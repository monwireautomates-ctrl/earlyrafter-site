Where the data comes from

Every issue is built from development applications published on the NSW Planning Portal, the state government's official system for lodging DAs. We check it daily, pick out every application that includes a swimming pool, and sort them by region and type of job.

It covers DAs lodged through the Planning Portal. Pools approved through other routes, such as complying development certificates, aren't included yet.
